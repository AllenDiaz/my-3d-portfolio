## **CUSTOMIZATION TIPS**

1. **Research the Company:**
   - Visit their website, read recent blog posts, check LinkedIn
   - Understand their products, mission, and tech stack
   - Find common connections or shared values

2. **Analyze the Job Description:**
   - Highlight key requirements and nice-to-haves
   - Match your experience to their specific needs
   - Use similar terminology they use in job posting

3. **Be Specific:**
   - Replace generic statements with concrete examples
   - Use metrics and numbers when possible (10,000+ invoices, 95% accuracy)
   - Reference specific technologies they mention

4. **Show Enthusiasm:**
   - Be genuine about why you want this specific role
   - Connect their mission to your career goals
   - Demonstrate you've done your homework

5. **Keep It Concise:**
   - For email applications: 300-500 words
   - For portal submissions: Can be longer (800-1,200 words)
   - Always prioritize quality over quantity
