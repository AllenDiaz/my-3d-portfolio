## 💡 Pro Tips

### **Do's:**
✅ Research the company before sending (15-20 minutes minimum)  
✅ Match their tech stack in your skills section  
✅ Use specific numbers and metrics (10,000+, 95%, 70%)  
✅ Show genuine enthusiasm for their specific mission  
✅ Proofread multiple times (company names, technical terms)  
✅ Keep tone professional but personable  
✅ Mention specific products/projects they're working on  
✅ Save customized version with company name (e.g., `CoverLetter_Google.md`)

### **Don'ts:**
❌ Send template with **[BRACKETS]** unchanged  
❌ Use generic "Dear Hiring Manager" without trying to find name  
❌ Copy-paste same letter for every application  
❌ Focus only on what you want (emphasize what you offer)  
❌ Write more than 2 pages for email applications  
❌ Forget to update work preference (Remote/Hybrid/On-site)  
❌ Oversell or make claims you can't back up  
❌ Send without spell-checking company name and role title

## 📎 Attachment Best Practices

When attaching your cover letter:

**File Naming:**
- ✅ `AllenDiaz_CoverLetter_CompanyName.pdf`
- ✅ `Allen_Diaz_Cover_Letter_Position.pdf`
- ❌ `cover_letter.pdf`
- ❌ `final_version_2.pdf`

**Format:**
- **PDF** (preferred) - Maintains formatting
- **DOCX** (if requested) - Some systems require Word
- **Plain text** (in email body) - For quick applications

**Resume + Cover Letter:**
- Attach both as separate files
- OR combine into one PDF (Cover Letter as page 1, Resume as pages 2-3)
- Always name files professionally
